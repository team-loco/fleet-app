## Details

1. **Flux failure flaps.** Polling the live snapshot every 10 s: `broken-kustomization` was missing
   from Problems on fleet-a at 0–30 s and again at 70 s, on fleet-b until 50 s. While it is out,
   Flock home's Flux column read `‖ 2/3` for fleet-b against five objects on GitOps.
   `kubectl` shows `Ready=False/ArtifactFailed` throughout; the summary turns the retry's
   Reconciling/stale-generation window into Progress. Repro: the ignored
   `live_flux_failures_stay_flagged_for_two_minutes`. Screens: `flock-overview-dark.png`,
   `flock-problems-dark.png`, `fleet-b-namespaces-light.png` (Kustomizations without a badge).
2. **Suspended 0s.** `changed_at` reads `managedFields`, which every watch store now drops.
   `fleet-a-helmreleases-search-search-indexer-dark.png` (Other clusters), `flock-problems-dark.png`.
3. **Disabled primary.** `fleet-a-helmreleases-search-search-indexer-dark.png`.
4. **Short names.** `fleet-a-compare-checkout-api-dark.png` ("Apply b value to a…"),
   `flock-changes-dark.png` (lane headers `a` `b`), every tab label.
5. **Silent loading.** Rollout history and image on fleet-b workloads
   (`fleet-b-deployments-checkout-api-dark.png`), Recent GitOps changes on Flock home, HelmRelease
   Values, Namespaces `0.0 cores` until the first pod-metrics poll, "Nothing matches this filter."
   under Managed objects with the All filter (`fleet-b-kustomizations-flux-system-broken-kustomization-dark.png`),
   and a Flux list header saying "0 objects" over skeleton rows.
6. **Row menu focus.** A right click opened the menu on mouse down and the table took focus back.
   Found by `live_every_inspector_row_menu_filter_and_namespace_picker_opens`; fixed in #199 with
   `a_right_clicked_row_menu_takes_the_keyboard`.
7. **Scenario drift.** `Pod/apps/unschedulable` cannot take the renamed `flock.dev/scenario` selector
   in place; the running clusters still have `fleet-nonexistent-path` and `--fleet-invalid-flag`.
8. **Truncation.** `fleet-b-deployments-stripe-webhook-dark.png` (`CreateContainerC…` next to empty
   CPU/Mem), `fleet-a-helmreleases-payments-checkout-api-dark.png` (`#11…`).
9. **Pods dock.** `fleet-a-pods-dark.png`.
10. **Diff hunks.** `flock-changes-dark.png` (28 → 48).
11. **Wording and units.** `×`, `Ctrs`, `Mem`, `dim rev = behind HEAD`, `filter /regex/`,
    `Terminal debug`, `podinfo · *`, `Metrics source: auto`, `1 / 7.8 GiB`, `0.0 cores`,
    `chart podinfo ·`.

Checked and fine: light mode and the Teal / Orange highlights keep selection, nav and status
colours legible on every page; nav counts and error badges stayed present across 84 sidebar
visits, tab switches, a split, ⌘[ / ⌘] and a minute on a live page; no unprovoked toasts; no page
